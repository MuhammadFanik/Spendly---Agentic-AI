Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, save your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. Passwords are stored in a protected form rather than as plain text. We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, but no system is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where the law requires. Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to ask for your account and data to be deleted, please get in touch with the Spendly team.
