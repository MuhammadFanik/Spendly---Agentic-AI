Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to run Spendly: to sign you in, save your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. Passwords are stored in a protected form rather than as plain text. We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, but no system is completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it if required by law.

5. Cookies

Spendly may use cookies, which are small files stored in your browser, to keep you signed in and make the service work properly.

6. Your Rights

You can view and edit the expenses you have entered. You may also ask us to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continuing to use Spendly after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please get in touch with the Spendly team.
